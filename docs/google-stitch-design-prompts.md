# Google Stitch Design Prompts

This document contains design prompts for Google Stitch, one screen at a time. Google Stitch is used as a design generator, not a code generator.

---

## Prompt 1 — Hotel Homepage

Design a homepage for a single boutique hotel. Full-width hero image of the hotel exterior or lobby with the hotel name, tagline, and a prominent "Check Availability" search bar (check-in, check-out, guests). Below: a "Why Stay With Us" section with 3–4 highlights (spa, restaurant, location), a photo gallery grid, room preview cards, guest reviews carousel, and a footer with contact info, address, and social links. Elegant, warm, and inviting color palette.

---

## Prompt 2 — Room Listing / Availability Page

Design a room availability page for a single hotel. At the top, a sticky date selector with check-in, check-out, and guests. Below, a grid of available room cards for the selected dates. Each card shows room photo, room name, bed type, max occupancy, amenities icons, price for the full stay, and a "Select Room" button. Include a "No availability" empty state and filter chips for room categories (Standard, Deluxe, Suite, Family).

---

## Prompt 3 — Room Detail Page

Design a room detail page for a single hotel room type. Large image gallery, room title, capacity, bed configuration, room size, amenities list, description, and cancellation policy. Right sidebar with a booking summary: selected dates, number of nights, price breakdown, taxes, total, and "Book This Room" CTA. Include a "You may also like" section with other room types. Clean, spacious layout with hotel-branded accent color.

---

## Prompt 4 — Guest Booking Page

Design a booking page where a guest enters their details after selecting a room. Two-column layout: left side has a booking summary card (room photo, room name, dates, guests, price) and a form with fields for first name, last name, email, phone, special requests, and estimated arrival time. Right side shows total price breakdown, accepted payment methods, and a "Proceed to Payment" CTA. Include trust signals: free cancellation badge, secure payment icon, and 24/7 front desk contact.

---

## Prompt 5 — Checkout / Payment Page

Design a secure checkout page for a single hotel booking. Left column: booking summary, guest details read-only, and a Stripe-style card payment form (card number, expiry, CVC). Right column: order summary showing nightly rate × nights, taxes, resort fees, total amount, and the hotel cancellation policy. Add a prominent "Complete Payment" CTA, trust badges, and a small hotel logo. Professional, calm design with soft blue or green accents.

---

## Prompt 6 — Booking Confirmation Page

Design a booking confirmation page for a single hotel. Large success checkmark, confirmation message "Your reservation is confirmed," booking reference number, hotel name, room type, check-in/check-out dates, guest count, total paid. Action buttons: "View My Booking," "Download PDF," "Add to Calendar," "Contact Hotel." Include a "Prepare for your stay" section with check-in time, amenities, and directions. Warm, reassuring design.

---

## Prompt 7 — Guest Dashboard / My Bookings

Design a guest dashboard for a single hotel. Sidebar with links: My Bookings, Profile, Contact Hotel. Main area shows an "Upcoming Stay" hero card with hotel image, dates, room type, status badge, and actions: "View Details," "Modify," "Cancel." Below, a "Booking History" list with past stays, dates, and "Book Again" buttons. Include an empty state for new guests with a CTA to explore rooms.

---

## Prompt 8 — Admin Dashboard Overview

Design an admin dashboard for a single hotel. Sidebar menu: Dashboard, Bookings, Rooms, Availability Calendar, Guests, Settings. Main area shows summary cards: today's check-ins, tonight's occupancy, revenue this month, pending bookings. Below, a recent bookings table and a small occupancy trend chart. Professional, clean admin UI with status color coding: green confirmed, orange pending, red cancelled.

---

## Prompt 9 — Admin Booking Management

Design an admin bookings page for a single hotel. A filterable table showing: booking ID, guest name, room type, check-in date, check-out date, total, payment status, and booking status. Include action buttons to view details, confirm, check in, check out, or cancel. Add a search bar and date filter. At the top, show quick stats: total bookings, arrivals today, departures today.

---

## Prompt 10 — Admin Room & Availability Calendar

Design an admin room management and availability calendar for a single hotel. Room types listed on the left, calendar grid on the right showing 30 days. Each cell shows remaining inventory and price for that date. Admins can click a date cell to edit availability count or set a special price. Include buttons to "Block All Rooms" or "Release Rooms" for a date range. Clear color coding: available green, blocked red, low stock yellow.

---

## Prompt 11 — Mobile Design System

Design a mobile-first design system for a single hotel reservation app. Include: color palette inspired by a boutique hotel brand (primary, accent, success, warning, neutral grays), typography scale, button styles, form inputs, date picker, cards, badges, modals, and toast notifications. Show examples for guest screens and admin screens. Use rounded corners, soft shadows, and accessible touch targets. Include a bottom navigation bar for guests and a collapsible sidebar for admin.

---

## Prompt 12 — Login & Register Pages

Design login and register pages for a single hotel reservation website. Clean, centered card layout on a subtle hotel-branded background. Login form: email, password, "Remember me," "Forgot password?" link, and social login buttons (Google, Apple). Register form: full name, email, phone, password, confirm password, terms checkbox. Both have clear CTAs and a link to switch between login/register. Minimal, elegant, and trustworthy.

---

## Prompt 13 — Hotel Amenities & Facilities Page

Design an amenities and facilities page for a single hotel. Hero section with a headline like "Everything you need for a perfect stay." Below, a bento-style grid of amenity cards: free Wi-Fi, swimming pool, spa & wellness, restaurant & bar, fitness center, free parking, airport shuttle, pet-friendly, business center, room service. Each card has an icon, title, short description, and a photo. Include opening hours for facilities like spa and restaurant where relevant.

---

## Prompt 14 — Photo Gallery Page

Design a full photo gallery page for a single hotel. Use a masonry or grid layout with categories: All, Exterior, Rooms, Dining, Pool & Spa, Events. Clicking a photo opens a lightbox modal with navigation arrows and image caption. Include a "Book Your Stay" floating CTA bar. Clean, immersive, and image-focused design with minimal UI chrome.

---

## Prompt 15 — Location & Contact Page

Design a location and contact page for a single hotel. Left side: large map placeholder with a hotel pin, full address, directions link, distance to airport/train station, nearby attractions list. Right side: contact form with name, email, phone, message, and "Send Inquiry" CTA. Below, contact cards for phone, email, WhatsApp, and front desk hours. Professional and easy to read.

---

## Prompt 16 — Guest Reviews Page

Design a reviews and ratings page for a single hotel. Top section shows average rating, total reviews count, and a rating breakdown by category: cleanliness, service, location, comfort, value. Below, a grid of review cards with guest avatar, name, rating, stay date, room type, and review text. Include a "Write a Review" CTA for past guests and filter options: most recent, highest rated, lowest rated.

---

## Prompt 17 — Admin Guest Management

Design an admin guest management page for a single hotel. A searchable table of guests with columns: name, email, phone, total bookings, last stay date, loyalty status. Clicking a row opens a guest detail side panel showing booking history, preferences, and notes. Include an "Add Guest" button and export option. Clean, data-dense admin UI.

---

## Prompt 18 — Admin Room Type & Pricing Setup

Design an admin page for managing room types and pricing for a single hotel. List of room type cards with thumbnail, name, base price per night, capacity, number of rooms, and status toggle. Clicking a card opens an edit modal with fields for name, description, amenities, photos upload area, base price, extra guest fee, and weekend/surcharge settings. Include a "Add Room Type" button and a preview of how it appears to guests.

---

## Prompt 19 — Admin Hotel Settings & Policies

Design a hotel settings page for admin. Sections: Hotel Info (name, address, contact, description, photos), Policies (check-in/check-out times, cancellation policy, pet policy, child policy), Payment Settings (accepted methods, currency, tax rate), Email Templates preview, and Users & Roles (add/remove staff, assign roles). Use form cards with clear section headers and a "Save Changes" sticky footer.

---

## Prompt 20 — Payment Failed / Retry Page

Design a payment failed page for a single hotel booking. Friendly error illustration, message "We couldn't process your payment," and a clear reason placeholder (card declined, expired, etc.). Show the booking summary again and provide a "Retry Payment" CTA, "Change Payment Method" option, and a "Cancel Booking" secondary link. Keep it calm and reassuring, not alarming.

---

## Prompt 21 — Cancellation & Modification Flow

Design a booking cancellation and modification flow for guests. A "Manage Booking" page where guests enter booking reference and email. Then a booking detail view with options to "Change Dates" or "Cancel Booking." Change dates shows a calendar with availability and price difference. Cancellation shows policy summary and refund estimate with a final "Confirm Cancellation" button and reason dropdown. Include clear confirmation modals.

---

## Prompt 22 — Email Template Designs

Design a set of email templates for a single hotel reservation system: booking confirmation email, payment receipt, pre-arrival reminder, booking modification confirmation, cancellation confirmation, and review request email. Each template includes hotel logo, guest name, booking reference, dates, room type, total, hotel address, contact info, and a clear CTA button. Mobile-friendly width, clean typography, and hotel-branded colors.

---

## Prompt 23 — Empty States & Error Pages

Design empty states and error pages for the single hotel website: 404 page with a hotel-themed illustration and "Back to homepage" CTA, no availability empty state with suggested alternative dates, no bookings empty state for new guest dashboard, no search results page, and generic error fallback with retry button. Use friendly illustrations and clear next steps.

---

## Prompt 24 — Loading Skeletons & Micro-Interactions

Design loading skeletons and micro-interactions for the single hotel reservation app. Include skeleton states for: hotel card, room card, booking summary, dashboard table, and calendar grid. Also design hover states for buttons and cards, focus states for form inputs, a date picker open animation, toast notifications for success/error, and a booking progress indicator animation. Keep motion subtle and purposeful.
